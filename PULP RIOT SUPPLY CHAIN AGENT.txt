A. CONTROL TOWER & INSTANT ANSWERS
================================================================================

A1 [P0] - Ask the Agent Anything
Description: Type simple questions about stock or risk, and get instant answers with data source and freshness tags.

A2 [P0] - Quick Stock Checker
Description: Select any product, warehouse, or sales channel to immediately check stock levels and forecast status.

A3 [P0] - Executive Summary Dashboard
Description: View yesterday Quick commerce sales, stockout risks, excess stock, fruit pulp supplies, and urgent approvals in one screen.

A4 [P0] - Regional Risk Heatmap
Description: A color-coded grid (Red/Yellow/Green) displaying risk across 5 flavors, 9 warehouses, and key ingredients.

A5 [P0] - Data Source Trust Badges
Description: Labels every number clearly as real fact, calculation, or estimate, along with when it was last updated.

A6 [P0] - Top 5 Daily Action Items
Description: Lists the 5 most urgent tasks ranked by financial impact so managers know what to solve first.

A7 [P1] - Weekly Review One-Pager
Description: Automatically exports a concise one-page summary for weekly team meetings to replace outdated monthly packs.

A8 diagram displaying a depot rebalancing map showing five feasible lanes designed to transfer stock from surplus donor depots to shortage depots

A9 [P0] - End-to-End Supply Chain "Pulse" Sankey FlowDescription: Displays a single horizontal flow diagram tracking volume from Suppliers (Raw Pulp kg) $\rightarrow$ Plants & Co-Packer (Bottles) $\rightarrow$ In-Transit Trucks $\rightarrow$ 9 Depots $\rightarrow$ 3 Channels (GT / MT / QC).   Pictorial Mechanics: Line thickness scales with bottle volume, while nodes light up Red / Amber / Green at active bottlenecks (such as a red landslide block on the passionfruit highway or an amber 60-day shelf-life filter in front of Modern Trade and Quick Commerce).

True values, Upper end, middle-end, low-end forecasts

B3 [P0] - Daily Quick Commerce Sales Monitor
Description: Tracks yesterday's sales from quick-delivery apps (Blinkit, Zepto, Swiggy) against weekly averages.

B4 [P0] - Spike Alarm
Description: Triggers instant alerts when online orders surge (1.5x, 2x, or 3x baseline) and calculates money at stake.

B8 [P1] - New Product Sales Predictor
Description: Predicts sales for brand-new flavors with no history by modeling them after similar existing flavors.

B9 [P1] - Marketing Campaign Calendar
Description: Mentions upcoming promotions and influencer campaigns so planners can prepare for expected demand jumps.(not text heavy)

B10 [P0] - Forecast Accuracy Scorecard
Description: Compares past predictions against actual sales to highlight overestimate or underestimate trends by warehouse.

B11 [P1] - Plain-English Sales Explainer
Description: Translates complex statistical changes into simple notes like: "Dragon Passion is selling at 4x normal speed (+300%), risking Rs. X loss."
Highlight this 1 line and simple line graph(true vs predicted(high,low,medium) of the particular product selected in B4

C. INVENTORY & WAREHOUSE MANAGEMENT
================================================================================

C1 [P0] - Live Warehouse Stock Table
Description: Displays the most recently reported inventory across all 9 warehouses and 5 flavors.bar chart displays the non-interchangeable stock breakdown by location—including plant finished goods, in transit, depot, and raw pulp—across five different product varieties.

C2 [P0] - Real-Time Inventory Estimate
Description: Estimates exact stock levels today between weekly reports by subtracting recent sales from shipments. 

C3 [P0] - Stock Location Breakdown
Description: Separates inventory by factory stock, items in trucks, warehouse stock, and raw fruit pulp.

C4 [P0] - Days-of-Supply Counter
Description: Calculates how many days current warehouse stock will last based on expected daily sales.

C5 [P0] - Stockout Date Predictor
Description: Shows the exact calendar date a warehouse will run out of stock under normal and high demand.

C6 [P0] - Smart Safety Stock Targets
Description: Replaces rigid rules with custom stock buffers based on lead times, shelf life, and demand jumps.

C7: sellable-life clock (P0): Track bottle age and show days left until stock becomes unsellable to MT/QC (60-day rejection rule on a 90-day life). Highlight days left

C10 [P1] - Oldest-Stock First Priority
Description: Recommends shipping products closest to expiry first (FEFO) to eliminate warehouse waste.

C12 [P0] - Stock Transfer Preview
Description: Shows exact stock buffers for both sending and receiving warehouses before moving stock.

C15 [P0] - Regional Demand Shift Alert
Description: Flags regional warehouses where actual consumer demand is diverging from monthly supply plans.


Interactive pictorial representation for C1,C2,C4,C5,C6eft Side (Vertical Liquid Tank for C1 & C2): Shows a warehouse tank where the ghost outline marks Last Week's Reported Stock (C1), the green "+" arrow shows Receipts Arrived, the red "−" arrow shows Estimated Sales Since Report, and the solid liquid level shows Today's Nowcast (C2). A horizontal dashed line across the tank marks the Smart Safety Stock Target (C6) vs. the old flat 21-day rule.   Right Side (Horizontal Burn-Down Timeline Bar for C4 & C5): Projects a calendar bar forward from today showing Days of Cover (C4), with an amber flag at the Earliest-Likely Stockout Date ($P10$) and a red flag at the Expected Stockout Date ($P50$) (C5).

C16 [P0] - Root-Cause Domino & Impact Map
Description: Traces domino effects across the business (e.g., late online spike signal -> depleted warehouse stock -> empty retail shelf). Shows both how sales spikes drain stock and how stockouts hide true customer demand) label should be easy to understand for user

C17 [P0] - Sales Surge Simulator
Description: Interactive slider (1x to 4x surge) showing how fast each warehouse runs out of stock during a spike.

C18 [P0] - Information Delay Cost Clock
Description: Tracks days elapsed between a store surge and planner awareness, calculating only the financial cost per day of delay.

C21 [P1] - Mitigation "Speed vs. Cost" Quadrant Bubble ChartDescription: Visually compares the 4 ways to fix a warehouse shortage (Inter-Depot Transfer, Plant Replenishment, Co-Packer Order, or Do Nothing).   Pictorial Mechanics: Plots each option as a bubble where the X-axis = Arrival Date vs. Depot Stockout Date, Y-axis = Cost in ₹ per Bottle, and Bubble Size = Bottles Delivered. Any option arriving after the warehouse's $P10$ stockout date falls into a shaded red "Arrives Too Late" zone
